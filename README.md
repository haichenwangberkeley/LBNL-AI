# LBNL AI

A standalone page with three copy-and-paste setup prompts for agentic AI assistants (Codex, with notes for Claude Code):

1. **Indico OneStop server**: build a local CERN Indico meeting portal and verify retrieval.
2. **Mattermost channel authentication**: authorize a connector, verify the account, and read a channel.
3. **Zoom transcript, summary, and email**: connect Zoom and Gmail, retrieve a transcript, and prepare a draft.

Open `index.html` in a browser (no build, no dependencies). Each slide has a **Copy prompt** button; use the arrow keys to change slides.

These prompts guide authorization and setup. They do not grant access to any service, and they never ask for passwords or tokens in chat.
